# RESEARCH RADAR: 10–15 Minute Pitch (In Plain, Simple English)
> **Designed for pitches, presentations, hackathon judging, and project defenses. No confusing academic jargon—just clear, impactful, and memorable points.**

---

## 🎯 The One-Sentence Hook
> *"Research Radar turns 3 months of painful research paper reading into 10 minutes of crystal-clear answers, side-by-side comparisons, and ready-to-run experiments."*

---

## Slide 1: Title & Introduction (1 Minute)
- **Title**: **RESEARCH RADAR** — The Smart Co-Pilot for Scientific & Engineering Literature
- **Speaker Script**:
  > *"Good morning/afternoon, everyone. My name is [Your Name], and today I want to ask you a simple question:  
  > If you’ve ever had to write a research paper or do a literature review, how much time did you spend downloading dozens of PDFs, skimming 30-page documents, and manually copying numbers into an Excel spreadsheet?  
  > For most students, professors, and researchers, that process takes **weeks or even months**.  
  > Today, we are introducing **Research Radar**—an intelligent cloud platform that turns that entire painful process into a 2-second search."*

---

## Slide 2: The Problem (2–3 Minutes)
### *"Why is Doing Research So Painful Today?"*

Explain the **3 Big Headaches** that every researcher faces:

1. **Information Overload**:
   - Over **3 million research papers** are published every single year.
   - You go to Google Scholar, search for a topic, and you get **5,000 PDF results**. Where do you even begin?
2. **The "Excel Nightmare"**:
   - Researchers currently have to open 20 different PDFs, hunt for the test results, and manually type them into a spreadsheet to see who got better results. It is slow, boring, and error-prone.
3. **ChatGPT Hallucinates & Lies**:
   - Many people try to ask AI models like ChatGPT. But general AI often **makes up fake citations, fake paper titles, and wrong numbers**. In real science and engineering, you cannot submit fake numbers.
4. **Missed Contradictions**:
   - Paper A says a technology works at 95% efficiency. Paper B tested it under real heat and says it fails completely. Researchers often miss these contradictions until their own physical experiment fails in the lab.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — Meet Research Radar (2 Minutes)
### *"Like Google Maps, but for Scientific Knowledge"*

- **What It Does**:
  - Instead of giving you a giant list of links to read, Research Radar gives you **structured answers**.
  - You enter any topic (across Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, or Chemical Engineering).
  - In seconds, it builds an interactive **Research Map** connecting all relevant peer-reviewed papers.
  - It pulls out the **exact numbers and benchmarks** and puts them in a **side-by-side comparison table** (just like comparing two smartphones on Amazon!).
  - It shows you the **exact verbatim quote and page number** from the paper so you never have to worry about AI hallucinations.
  - It automatically flags **scientific contradictions** between researchers.
  - And best of all: it tells you **what is still unsolved (the Research Gaps)** and gives you a step-by-step experiment plan to test it.

---

## Slide 4: How We Solved It — Cloud Architecture (2 Minutes)
### *"Built on Salesforce Enterprise Cloud PaaS"*

Keep it simple and impressive:

- **Enterprise Reliability**: We didn't build this on a fragile local server that crashes. We built it natively on the **Salesforce Lightning Platform (PaaS)**—the same cloud trusted by Fortune 500 companies.
- **Relational Integrity (No Orphaned Records)**: Every paper, metric, verified finding, contradiction, and research gap is relationally linked. If a paper is updated, all numbers update in real time across the platform.
- **100% Fact-Checked**: Every single claim stores a verified DOI link and an exact page number. Zero hallucinations. Zero fake data.
- **Lightning Fast UI**: Built using modern **Lightning Web Components (LWC)** with a clean, high-contrast black-and-white design that feels like a precision scientific instrument.

## Slide 5: Live Product Walkthrough / Demo (4–5 Minutes)
### *"What the User Actually Experiences"*

Take the audience through the 4 key screens:

1. **The Executive Dashboard**:
   - Clean KPI cards at the top show total research maps, indexed papers, verified findings, and open gaps.
   - Quick branch filter buttons let you switch between **Chemical, Mechanical, Civil, EEE, ECE, or All Disciplines** in one click.
2. **One-Click Topic Synthesis**:
   - Type in a topic like *"Serverless cold-start latency"* or *"GaN microgrid power conversion"*.
   - Click **Generate Research Map**. In 2 seconds, the live pipeline creates a synthesized research cluster.
3. **The Literature Comparison Matrix**:
   - Show the side-by-side table.
   - Point out how easy it is to compare latency, power efficiency, space-time yield, or material strength without reading 50 pages of text.
4. **The Evidence Inspector**:
   - Click on any finding. Show the exact quote from the paper, the page number, and the confidence rating.
5. **The Research Gap Kanban Board**:
   - Show the Trello-style board.
   - Explain: *"This is where ideas turn into real projects. When Research Radar finds an unsolved question, it creates a card with a drafted experimental protocol so researchers can immediately begin validation in the lab."*
